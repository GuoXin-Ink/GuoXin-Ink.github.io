I am a PhD candidate at the Institute of Geodesy at Graz University of Technology (TU Graz), supervised by [Prof. Tobias Bolch](https://tugraz.elsevierpure.com/de/persons/tobias-bolch/). I joined TU Graz in September 2025 and am a member of the [MountCryo team](https://www.mountcryo.org/). My doctoral research investigates rock glacier kinematics and climate sensitivity in alpine regions using multi-source remote sensing.

Before joining TU Graz, I received an M.S. in Remote Sensing (2025) and a B.S. in Surveying and Mapping Engineering (2022) from Southwest Jiaotong University. During this period, I was a member of Prof. Xiaowen Wang's [GoIMAGE research team](https://goimage.cn/). My master's research focused on comprehensive rock glacier inventorying and activity-state classification using machine learning and multi-source remote sensing.

My research combines satellite InSAR, optical imagery, deep learning, and geospatial analysis to map and monitor rock glaciers. I am particularly interested in data-driven analysis of permafrost geomorphology and the response of alpine environments to climatic change.
