From 14 to 16 June 2024, I attended the **First Symposium on Radar Interferometry** in Wuhan and presented my work, **“Detecting intact and relict rock glaciers by combining InSAR and optical imagery: a machine-learning approach.”**

The presentation introduced a workflow that combines InSAR-derived motion information with geomorphological features from optical imagery. Machine learning was used to distinguish intact and relict rock glaciers and support efficient inventorying over large mountain regions.

The symposium covered InSAR systems, methods, data processing, and Earth-observation applications. It also provided a valuable opportunity to discuss remote sensing research with other participants. More information is available on the [official symposium website](https://insar2024.aconf.cn/).
