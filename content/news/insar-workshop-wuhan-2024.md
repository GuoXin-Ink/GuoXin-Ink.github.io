From 14 to 16 June 2024, I attended the **First Symposium on Radar Interferometry** in Wuhan and presented my research on identifying rock glacier states with multi-source remote sensing.

## Research presented

**Detecting intact and relict rock glaciers by combining InSAR and optical imagery: a machine-learning approach**

The presentation introduced a workflow that combines InSAR-derived motion information with geomorphological features from optical imagery. Machine learning was used to distinguish intact and relict rock glaciers and support efficient inventorying over large mountain regions.

## Meeting context

The symposium covered InSAR systems and missions, theory and methods, data-processing technologies, and Earth-observation applications. It provided a valuable opportunity to discuss remote sensing methods with researchers working across geodesy, natural hazards, and cryosphere change.

More information is available on the [official symposium website](https://insar2024.aconf.cn/).
