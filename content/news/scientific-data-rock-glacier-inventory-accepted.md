On 28 July 2026, our paper, **“Rock glaciers of the Southeastern Tibetan Plateau: A large-scale inventory derived from deep learning and high-resolution imagery,”** was accepted for publication in *Scientific Data*. I am the first author of this work.

Using 4.7 m Planet Basemap imagery and a SegFormer model trained on 19,096 samples, we compiled a detailed inventory covering 545,400 km² of the Southeastern Tibetan Plateau. After human-in-the-loop quality control and UAV-supported field checks, the final dataset contains 47,916 rock glaciers and provides a baseline for permafrost monitoring, hydrological modelling, and geohazard assessment.

The article was published open access on 5 August 2026 and is available on the [publisher's website](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-08019-z) under DOI [10.1038/s41597-026-08019-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-08019-z).
