On 20 May 2025, I successfully defended my master's thesis at the Faculty of Geosciences and Engineering, Southwest Jiaotong University.

## Thesis

**Methods for Large-Area Rock Glacier Inventorying and Activity-State Identification Based on Remote Sensing Imagery and Machine Learning**

Supervised by Prof. Xiaowen Wang, this research developed a workflow that combines remote sensing imagery, machine learning, and kinematic information to support large-area rock glacier inventorying and activity-state identification.

The defense marked the completion of an important stage of my master's research and laid the foundation for my continued work on rock glacier mapping, dynamics, and climate response.
