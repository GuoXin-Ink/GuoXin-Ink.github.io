From 26 to 27 February 2026, I attended the **29th Alpine Glaciology Meeting (AGM 2026)** in Milan. The meeting brought together researchers working on glaciers, permafrost, snow, and high-mountain environmental change.

## Meeting themes

The programme focused on the rapid transformation of the Alpine cryosphere and its scientific and societal implications. Topics included glacier retreat, hydrological change, permafrost-related slope instability, glacial hazards, atmospheric pollution recorded in ice, and the use of field observations, remote sensing, numerical modelling, and palaeoclimate records.

The first day was hosted by the University of Milano-Bicocca and the second by the University of Milan “La Statale”. The meeting provided a valuable opportunity to follow current Alpine cryosphere research and exchange ideas with researchers at different career stages.

Further information is available on the [official AGM 2026 website](https://sites.google.com/unimib.it/eurocold/agm-29th-2026).
