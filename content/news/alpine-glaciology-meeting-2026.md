From 26 to 27 February 2026, I attended the **29th Alpine Glaciology Meeting (AGM 2026)** in Milan. The meeting brought together researchers working on glaciers, permafrost, snow, and high-mountain environmental change.

The programme focused on the rapid transformation of the Alpine cryosphere, including glacier retreat, hydrological change, permafrost-related slope instability, glacial hazards, and new developments in field observation, remote sensing, and modelling. The first day was hosted by the University of Milano-Bicocca and the second by the University of Milan “La Statale”.

The meeting was a valuable opportunity to follow current Alpine cryosphere research and exchange ideas with other researchers. Further information is available on the [official AGM 2026 website](https://sites.google.com/unimib.it/eurocold/agm-29th-2026). The cover image is the official graphic published by the organisers.
