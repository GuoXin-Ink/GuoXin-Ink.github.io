From 25 to 31 August 2026, I joined our team for several days of fieldwork in **Hohe Tauern National Park, Austria**.

We visited several high-alpine permafrost and rock glacier sites, including the Dösen Rock Glacier and Hinteres Langtalkar Rock Glacier. Observing these landforms and surface conditions directly in the field helped me connect remote-sensing observations with the geomorphological features on the ground. This was particularly valuable for my research on rock glacier kinematics using InSAR and other remote-sensing techniques.

Beyond the scientific work, I greatly enjoyed the discussions, hiking, and time spent together in the alpine environment. Many thanks to Tobias, Peter, Viktor, Wieland, and Nina for the organization, discussions, support, and excellent company during the fieldwork.
