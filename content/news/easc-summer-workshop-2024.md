I was selected to participate in the **EASC Student Summer Workshop 2024**, organised by the Graduate Division of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at The Chinese University of Hong Kong from 9 to 11 July 2024.

The three-day programme included faculty research seminars, poster presentations, academic discussions, meetings with professors and students, and an introduction to doctoral study and the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme. It was a valuable opportunity to learn about interdisciplinary Earth system science and exchange ideas with faculty members and other prospective doctoral researchers.

Details are available on the [official workshop page](https://www.ees.cuhk.edu.hk/news/easc-online-student-summer-workshop-2024/).
