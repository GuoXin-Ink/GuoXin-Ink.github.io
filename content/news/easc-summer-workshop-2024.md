I was selected to participate in the **EASC Student Summer Workshop 2024**, organised by the Graduate Division of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at The Chinese University of Hong Kong from 9 to 11 July 2024.

## Workshop experience

The three-day programme introduced the EASC PhD programme and brought participants into direct contact with current Earth system science research. It included:

- Research seminars given by faculty members
- Poster presentations and academic discussion
- Small-group and individual meetings with professors and students
- Information about doctoral study and the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme

The workshop offered a valuable opportunity to learn about interdisciplinary research spanning the geosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, and biosphere, while exchanging ideas with faculty members and other prospective doctoral researchers.

Details are available on the [official workshop page](https://www.ees.cuhk.edu.hk/news/easc-online-student-summer-workshop-2024/).
