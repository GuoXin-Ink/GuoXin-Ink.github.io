From 31 August to 7 September 2026, I participated in the **PermaCOST PhD Training School, “Tools for Tracking Periglacial Change: From Local to Regional Approaches,”** in Trafoi, Italy.

The programme combined lectures, hands-on exercises, and fieldwork on RGIK rock glacier inventorying, geomorphological mapping, DInSAR-based kinematic characterization, UAV and GNSS surveying, Structure from Motion, Electrical Resistivity Tomography, and ground truthing. Working across these methods provided valuable experience in connecting field observations and geophysical measurements with regional remote-sensing analyses.

The training school was also a great opportunity to exchange ideas with instructors and fellow doctoral researchers working on permafrost and periglacial environments. Further information is available on the [official RGIK event page](https://www.rgik.org/permacost-training-school/).
