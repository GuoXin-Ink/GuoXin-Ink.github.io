On 30 March 2026, our paper, **“Integrating optical and InSAR data for machine learning-based rock glacier activity classification: Model evaluation across mountain regions,”** was accepted for publication in *Geo-spatial Information Science*. I am the second author of this work.

## About the study

The study combines optical remote sensing and InSAR-derived motion information to evaluate machine-learning models for classifying rock glacier activity across different mountain regions. By testing model performance across contrasting study areas, the work examines how well data-driven classification approaches transfer between regional settings.

The article was published online on 16 April 2026 and is available through the [publisher's website](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10095020.2026.2654942).

**DOI:** [10.1080/10095020.2026.2654942](https://doi.org/10.1080/10095020.2026.2654942)
